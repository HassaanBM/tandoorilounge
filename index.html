Welcome to Tandoori Lounge: A Symphony of Flavors

At Tandoori Lounge, we celebrate the rich and diverse culinary heritage of India with a contemporary twist. Nestled in the heart of Austin, our restaurant is an ode to the vibrant flavors, age-old traditions, and warm hospitality of our homeland.
